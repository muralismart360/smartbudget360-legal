About SmartBudget360

SmartBudget360 helps you see where your money goes and stay inside your budget, without giving your financial data to anyone.

What it does

  • Record income and expenses in a few taps, including recurring payments
  • Plan monthly budgets by category and get alerts as you near a limit
  • Set savings goals and see how much to set aside each month
  • Understand your spending with insights, month-to-month comparisons and a month-end forecast
  • Create PDF and Excel reports, back up and restore your data, and import bank statements (CSV)
  • Use it in English, Telugu or Hindi, with any currency

Privacy first

Your transactions, budgets and goals are stored on your device. There is no account and no cloud sync, and the app works offline. You can optionally protect it with your phone's screen lock. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is and is not collected.

Free to use

SmartBudget360 is free. It may show advertisements to support development. Backing up and restoring your data never requires watching an ad.
