Privacy Policy for SmartBudget360

Effective Date: November 3, 2026

Last Updated: November 12, 2026

In short: Everything you enter into SmartBudget360 (transactions, budgets, goals, your name) is stored only on your device. We have no accounts and no servers, and we cannot see your data. The app works fully offline. The app uses the internet to show ads, to send anonymous crash reports and usage statistics (you can turn this off in Settings), and to open this policy online. Your financial data is never sent to us, to advertisers or to Google's analytics.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

SmartBudget360 stores the following locally on your device only:

  • Your name (optional, entered during setup)
  • Financial data you enter: income and expense transactions, categories, payment methods, monthly budgets, savings goals and recurring payments
  • Preferences: currency, language (English, Telugu or Hindi), light or dark theme, reminder times and on/off settings

This data lives in a private database inside the app. It is not uploaded anywhere by the app.

2. How the Information Is Used

  • To show your transactions, budgets, goals, insights, comparisons and reports inside the app
  • To calculate things such as monthly totals, savings rate and category changes. All calculations happen on your device
  • To send you local reminders you have switched on

3. Features That Use Your Device

  • Notifications: The daily reminder, budget alerts and upcoming-bill reminders are scheduled and shown by your device. No notification content leaves your device. The app asks for notification permission and may reschedule reminders after the phone restarts.
  • App lock (screen lock): If you turn on App lock, the app asks Android to verify you with your phone's own PIN, pattern, password or fingerprint. The app never sees or stores your PIN, pattern, password or biometric data. It only remembers that App lock is switched on.
  • Exporting and importing files: PDF and Excel reports, JSON backups and CSV bank statements are saved and read through Android's system file picker. The app can only read or write the file you choose. Imported statements are processed on your device and are not uploaded.
  • Reports and backups are not encrypted. Anyone who receives a file you export can read it. Choose where you save it and whom you share it with.
  • Sample data: The optional "Sample data" feature adds made-up demo entries to your device. It does not download anything and you can remove it at any time.
  • Android device backup: Your phone or Google account may back up app data as part of Android's own backup feature. That is controlled by your device settings, not by us.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Some versions of SmartBudget360 are supported by ads. When ads are enabled, the app may show:

  • a banner ad above the bottom navigation bar, and
  • an optional rewarded video ad before you create a PDF or Excel report. After one ad, report exports are free for a short time. Backups (JSON) and restoring never require an ad. If an ad cannot be loaded, your export still goes ahead.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process information such as your advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location it implies), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This is governed by Google's policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

We do not share your name, transactions, budgets, goals or any other financial data with Google or any advertiser.

  • Consent: Where the law requires it (for example the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app shows Google's consent form before any ads are requested.
  • Your controls: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in Android Settings under Google → Ads.
  • When the version you have installed shows no ads, none of the above applies and the app makes no advertising requests.

5. Crash Reports and Usage Statistics (Google Firebase)

To find and fix problems and to understand how the app is used, SmartBudget360 uses Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics, services of Google. They collect anonymous information such as:

  • crash and error reports (what went wrong inside the app, the app version, phone model and Android version)
  • which screens and features are used (for example "a report was exported" or "a budget was created"), how often, and whether people come back
  • approximate location (country or city, from the network connection), language, and a random app-installation identifier

This never includes your name, your transactions, amounts, categories, notes, budgets, goals, or any other financial data. We do not ask for your email or phone number and do not link this data to you.

  • You are in control: turn Settings → Share anonymous usage data off at any time. Collection stops straight away.
  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), nothing is collected until you give consent in the consent form.
  • Google processes this data under its own terms: Firebase privacy information. Analytics data is kept for 14 months.
  • Google signals and ad personalisation are not used for this data.

6. Other Network Use

  • The Privacy Policy and Terms of Service screens load the pages from our website. A built-in copy is shown if you are offline.
  • Links you tap (for example a store page) open in your browser or the relevant app.

SmartBudget360 does not include social media or payment services.

7. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: so ads can load and anonymous crash and usage data can be sent (if you allow it)
  • Notifications and run-at-startup: to deliver the reminders you turn on
  • Biometric / screen lock: so Android can show its own unlock prompt for App lock
  • Storage (older Android versions only): to save exported files where you choose

8. Data Storage and Security

  • Your data stays on your device until you delete it, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • Its security depends on your device's protections (screen lock, device encryption). Turning on App lock in Settings adds a further check when the app opens.
  • Because there is no cloud copy, we cannot recover your data. Make a backup from Settings → Export data and keep it somewhere safe. The app reminds you from time to time.

9. Data Deletion and Your Control

  • Delete individual transactions inside the app (swipe an entry in Activity; you can undo straight away).
  • Turn off Settings → Share anonymous usage data to stop crash and usage reports.
  • Clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app, to permanently erase everything the app stores.
  • Files you exported are yours; delete them from where you saved them.

10. Children's Privacy

SmartBudget360 is a personal finance tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are under the age of majority in your country, please use the app with a parent or guardian.

11. Your Rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data (the anonymous crash and usage data described above cannot be linked to you), there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You have full access to, and control of, everything stored in the app, and can export it (JSON, Excel or PDF) at any time. Requests about advertising or Firebase data are handled by Google under its privacy policy.

  • GDPR / UK GDPR: we do not process your personal data on servers; Google acts as an independent controller for advertising and Firebase data and consent is obtained where required.
  • CCPA and similar laws: we do not sell your personal information. Advertising data processed by Google is subject to Google's terms.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app's features change or the law requires. The "Last Updated" date above will change and the current version is always available in the app under Settings → Privacy Policy. If we ever add features that send your data off your device, we will ask for your permission first.
